Plain, lab only ⓘ
No encryption, anyone can connect. For a trusted lab network only.
Encrypted ⓘ
Clients verify the broker. Uses the local CA and server certificate.
Encrypted + passwords ⓘ
Encrypted, and each client needs a username and password.
Mutual TLS ⓘ
Encrypted, and each device needs its own client certificate, issued on the Broker screen. No passwords.

What Mutual TLS means. The broker proves who it is with its server certificate, and each device proves who it is with a client certificate you issue. Install CA.pem on the device together with its certificate. This is the strongest setup and needs no passwords. It will turn on "require client certificate" for this listener and use the local CA; the other listeners are not changed.

CancelNext

Privacy

MQTT Panel collects nothing. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting and no account. The app connects only to the brokers you configure and, if enabled above, to the release feed. Passwords, keys and passphrases are stored in the system keychain.

The lab-only skip-verification toggle is shown on here to demonstrate the warning in the title bar pill; it is off by default and resets at launch.
